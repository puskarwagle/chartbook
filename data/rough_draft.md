**Prefrontal Cortex ka mukhya karyaharu:**
*   **Karyakari niyantran (Executive Function):**
    Yojana banaune, nirnaya line
    ra jatil samasya haru hal garne.
*   **Working Memory:**
    Kunai kaam garirhanda aawashyak janakari lai
    kehi ber ka lagi dimag ma hold garera rakhne
    (jastai: hisab garda ank samjhine).
*   **Impulse Control:**
    Tatkal aaune aaveg wa ichhya lai rokne
    (jastai: ris uthda turuntai pratikriya nadine).
*   **Dhyan byabasthapan (Focus & Attention):**
    Kun kura ma dhyan dine
    ra kun kura lai bewasta (Ignore) garne bhanne tay garne.
*   **Bhawnatmak niyaman (Emotional Regulation):**
    Bhawna haru lai santulan ma rakhne.

*   **Dopamine ko abhav (Dopamine Deficiency):**

    Tyasaile sadharan ra jhyau lagdo kaam garna
    yo dimag le mandai mandaina.
    Yaslai sadhai kehi 'naya' wa 'uttejak' (Stimulating) kura chahinchha.
*   **Threshold ko samasya (Low Arousal Threshold):**
    Hamro dimag ki ta ati nai uttejit huncha (Hyperactive),
    ki ta ekdamai susta huncha (Under-aroused).
    Bich ko shanta ra ekagra awastha (Flow State) pauna
    dherai garo huncha.
*   **Interoception ko kami:**
    Aafno sharir ko antarik sanket haru (bhok, pyas, thakan)
    samay mai thaha napaunu.
    Yo pani PFC ra 'Insula' bich ko kamjor sambandha le garda huncha.
*   **Samay ko andhopan (Time Blindness):**
    Samay biteko pattai napaunu.
    Yo mastishka ko samay ganana garne bhag ma hune dhilaai le garda huncha.

  yo 'Self-medication' ho.

* **Bigriyeka sambandha haru:**

  Bhawana ma aaune utarchadhaw
  ra arthik bhadragol le garda
  lamo samaya samma sambandha tikauna garo huncha.
* **Berojgari ra arthik asthirta:**
  Uni haru le ausat manis bhanda kam kamaunchan.
  Jagir bata nikalne
  wa padhai pura nahune sambhawana dherai rahanchha.
* **Self-care ma samasya:**
  Daat majhne wa nuhaune jasta samanya kaam pani
  uni haru ka lagi pahad jastai hunchhan.
  Yo aalasya hoina,
  yo auta Neurological samasya ho.
* **Kanuni jhamela:**
  Jel ma raheka karib 25-40% manis ma
  ADHD bhaeko anuman garinchha.
  Hatar ma nirnaya garne bani (Impulsivity) le garda
  uni haru kanuni fanda ma parchhan.
* **Bidroh:**
  Varsau samma "ta alchi hos, murkha hos" bhanne shabda
  sunda sunda uni haru ma aakrosh janminchha.
  Kasai le tyo ris aafaitira modchhan (Self-harm),
  ta kasai le samaj tira.
* **Aatmahatya:**
  ADHD bhaeka haru ma
  aatmahatya ko prayas garne sambhawana 2-4 guna badhi huncha.
* **Choto aayu:**
  Research le dekhaunchha ki
  upachar nabhaeko gambhir ADHD le

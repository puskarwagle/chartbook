Global Aggregators & ToolsWhile no official WHO tracker exists for retail market approvals, several public databases compile registration data across multiple borders:WWAD (World Wide Approved Drugs Database): An academic, open-access database that compiles official drug registration records from regulatory agencies across 88 countries. It allows searches by Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) to see where a drug has secured active approval.  WHO Prequalification List: Managed by the WHO, but strictly covers priority medicines (like HIV, TB, or malaria treatments) evaluated for international procurement in developing nations. It does not track general retail commercial registrations (darta).WHO International Pharmacopoeia / eEML: Tracks whether a drug is on national "Essential Medicines Lists" across 135+ countries, which indicates clinical recommendations rather than commercial registration status.DrugBank Online: Aggregates regulatory status for the US, Canada, EU, and select international regions under its "Approved" or "Investigational" flags per drug entry.
